## 6. FAQ

**Section headline:** Questions? We've got answers

**Q: What ages is AnnaBer for?**
A: We teach kids from 7 to 16. Lessons are matched to your child's level, not just their age — so everyone learns at the right pace.

**Q: How long are the lessons?**
A: Individual sessions are 25 minutes. Group sessions are 45 minutes. Short enough to keep focus, long enough to make progress.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You can pause or stop your plan whenever you need to.

**Q: What if my child doesn't like their teacher?**
A: No problem — we'll match you with a different teacher. Finding the right fit is part of what we do.

**Q: Do you teach British or American English?**
A: Both. You choose the accent and curriculum that fits your goals — we'll make sure the teacher matches.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes — your first lesson is completely free. No card, no commitment. Just book and show up.
