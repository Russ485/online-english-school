# Ticket: Visual Assets Strategy

**Label:** wayfinder:grilling
**Map:** map-annaber-landing.md
**Status:** CLOSED

## Question

Decide the visual asset strategy for AnnaBer landing page.

## Resolution

**Hero:** Abstract illustration with playful geometric shapes (circles, rounded rectangles, stars) in the blue/coral palette. No literal imagery of kids.

**Benefits:** Phosphor icons only — no images per benefit. Clean and fast.

**Pricing:** No visual treatment beyond cards. Clean typography-driven pricing.

**How it works:** Numbered steps with Phosphor icons. Simple, scannable.

**Testimonials:** Placeholder avatar circles with initials. No stock photos of people.

**Overall style:** Abstract geometric illustration — rounded shapes, soft gradients within the blue/coral palette, playful but not childish. Generated via CSS/SVG or AI image tool.
